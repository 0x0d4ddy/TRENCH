CREDITS


THE ORIGINAL

  TRENCH started from "BODYCAM-Game - Increase maximum limit of players" by
  TRUEMODELOFTHEWORLD, released under the MIT License:

      github.com/TRUEMODELOFTHEWORLD/BODYCAM-Game---Increase-maximum-limit-of-players

  That mod solved the first hard part - getting a Bodycam host to accept more
  players than the game allows, and filling the teams with bots - and it solved
  it through reflection, without touching a single game file. Everything here is
  built on that foundation. Thank you.


WHAT WAS ADDED HERE

  Artillery: salvos fired from off the map, whistling in over several seconds,
  landing anywhere on the map, on the crowds, or on you. Damage through the
  game's own effects.

  The TRENCH panel: a control panel with its own sound engine, playing the
  incoming shells in 3D around you and following where you stand and look.

  Team sizes that hold for the whole match, match length, presets, an installer.
